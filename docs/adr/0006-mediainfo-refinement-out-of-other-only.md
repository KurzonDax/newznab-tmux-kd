# Mediainfo refines a release only out of `<root>/Other`, within its root — except audio-only content, which is refiled under Audio

Categorization runs at release creation and sees only group, name and poster. Obfuscated-name routing (ADR 0005) and the year-only movie fallback therefore park releases in a root's *Other* subcategory with no way to say more. Mediainfo arrives later, during additional post-processing, and carries hard evidence — resolution, container, codec, and whether there is a video track at all. Issue #66.

We decided on **Mediainfo refinement**: after mediainfo is recorded, a release sitting in Movies/Other, TV/Other, XXX/Other or Audio/Other is moved to a more specific subcategory of the **same root** using a conservative, fixed mapping (UHD by resolution, then DVD/Bluray by container, then codec, then HD/SD by resolution; audio format → MP3 / Lossless). Anything the mapping cannot decide stays in `<root>/Other`. The refinement runs inline in additional processing and is re-applied by every later category-write site whose result is an eligible `<root>/Other`; a backfill command covers the existing backlog. It is unconditional — no site or per-root toggle.

Deliberate choices a future reader may be tempted to "fix":

- **It only ever moves a release *out of* `<root>/Other`; it never corrects a specific subcategory.** A name-based Movies/HD stays Movies/HD even if mediainfo reports 2160p. Mediainfo here corrects the *absence* of evidence, not conflicting evidence. Overriding name-derived subcategories would make mediainfo the master signal for every release and turn a small refinement into a full re-categorization pass with a very different risk profile — a separate feature if ever wanted.
- **Video evidence never crosses a root.** XXX/Other stays under XXX, Movies/Other under Movies. Mediainfo can say "1080p HEVC", not "this is a TV episode rather than a film"; the root chosen from group/name remains the stronger content signal.
- **Audio-only content is the one cross-root exception.** A release in Movies/TV/XXX `Other` whose media has an audio track and no video track cannot be a movie, episode or XXX video, whatever group posted it, so it is refiled under Audio (MP3 / Lossless / Other by format). This is deliberately in tension with "the routed root is a floor" from ADR 0005 — the floor holds for video content only. The audio-tag rename path already did this when tags were complete; the refinement covers the rest, and does not depend on the music-rename setting, which governs renaming, not filing.
- **The mapping is deliberately incomplete.** WEB-DL, 3D, Foreign, Sport, Docu, VR, Pack, Imageset and the XXX Clip categories are not derived — they need source, language, duration or content signals mediainfo does not reliably give. Duration-based clip detection was considered and rejected: no other subcategory in the system is duration-based, and the thresholds would be guesses. TV has no DVD subcategory; a DVD source is standard definition by definition and lands in TV/SD via the resolution rule, which is intended, not an omission.
- **No toggle.** It cannot fire without mediainfo processing being on, and it only ever makes a category more specific within a root the operator already accepted (or moves audio into Audio); a knob would exist to be left alone.

Settled in a triage session on 2026-08-15. See `CONTEXT.md` for **Mediainfo refinement** and the amended **Obfuscated-name routing** vocabulary.
